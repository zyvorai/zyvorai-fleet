# Zyvor Fleet: support subscriptions

Zyvor Fleet is source-available software. A commercial license is required to run it (sales@zyvor.dev); a 30-day trial key is how to evaluate.

Teams that want supported releases and defined response targets can buy an annual **support subscription** from Zyvor.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Standard | Deployment and upgrade guidance, defined supported configuration | Business hours |
| Supported | Standard plus priority queue and incident triage | Business hours, faster response |
| Enterprise | Supported plus a named technical contact and 24x7 critical intake | One-hour initial response target for critical issues |

Support is sized per device fleet, with an annual minimum per plan.

Rates are below.

## Prices

Support is priced per managed device per year (Fleet, Relay, Device Agent, OTA and Nodra share one device ladder).

| Devices | USD, excl. tax | India, INR incl. 18% GST |
|---------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 1-999 | USD 18 per device per year | INR 1,784 per device per year |
| 1,000-9,999 | USD 12 per device per year | INR 1,189 per device per year |
| 10,000+ | USD 8 per device per year | INR 793 per device per year |

Annual minimum: USD 5,000 (INR 495,600 incl. 18% GST). Multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%. Minimums are annual contract values and apply after discounts.

Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev).

Buy / get a quote: <https://zyvor.dev/buy>. A 30-day trial key is available for production licences ([30-day PoC](https://zyvor.dev/poc)).

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Rights under the Zyvor Production Licence v2.0 continue as stated in that license.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
